Privacy Policy

Ingat Mantra (Penyihir vs Kucing) — Last updated: November 27, 2026

Summary: Ingat Mantra stores only the leaderboard data described in section 1 (your Discord username, profile photo, and score) when you choose to take part in the leaderboard. It does not collect, store, or share any other personal data.

1. Data Processed by the Game

This game is a Discord Activity that runs inside the Discord app. When you play it:

  • If you take part in the leaderboard, the game sends your Discord user ID, username, profile photo, and score to the developer's leaderboard server (hosted on the developer's VPS) and stores them there. Your username, photo, and score on the leaderboard are visible to all players. This only happens after you authorize the game in Discord (scope identify); if you do not authorize it, you play anonymously and nothing is stored.
  • Your game progress (level, score, bosses defeated) is stored on the leaderboard server only when you take part in the leaderboard; otherwise it lives only for the duration of your play session on your device and is lost when the game is closed.

2. Data We Do Not Collect

Except for the leaderboard data in section 1, we do not collect, store, or share:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or home address.
  • Your location, contacts, photos, or files from your device.
  • Tracking data, analytics, cookies, or advertising. This game contains no ads and uses no third-party tracking services.

3. Third Parties

The game files are hosted on GitHub Pages, and the game runs on the Discord platform. GitHub and Discord each have their own privacy policies governing the data they process as service providers:

  • GitHub Pages — GitHub Privacy Statement
  • Discord — Discord Privacy Policy

4. Children

This game is for a general audience and does not collect personal data from anyone, including children. If you are a minor, please play with a parent's or guardian's permission. Players under 18 should only take part in the leaderboard with a parent's or guardian's permission.

5. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version is always available on this page.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via Telegram: @Minatomitto. You can ask for your leaderboard entry to be deleted at any time via Telegram: @Minatomitto.
