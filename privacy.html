Privacy Policy

Ingat Mantra (Penyihir vs Kucing) — Last updated: November 26, 2026

Summary: Ingat Mantra does not collect, store, or share your personal data. The game runs entirely on your device.

1. Data Processed by the Game

This game is a Discord Activity that runs inside the Discord app. When you play it:

  • Discord may show your name and photo inside the game (through the Discord app authorization). This information is only used for display while you play and is not sent to any server operated by the developer — the developer operates no server that receives this data.
  • Your game progress (level, score, bosses defeated) is only stored for the duration of your play session on your device and is lost when the game is closed.

2. Data We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, store, or share:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or home address.
  • Your location, contacts, photos, or files from your device.
  • Tracking data, analytics, cookies, or advertising. This game contains no ads and uses no third-party tracking services.

3. Third Parties

The game files are hosted on GitHub Pages, and the game runs on the Discord platform. GitHub and Discord each have their own privacy policies governing the data they process as service providers:

  • GitHub Pages — GitHub Privacy Statement
  • Discord — Discord Privacy Policy

4. Children

This game is for a general audience and does not collect personal data from anyone, including children. If you are a minor, please play with a parent's or guardian's permission.

5. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version is always available on this page.

6. Contact
